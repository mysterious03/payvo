Pillars of Safety

Engineered with Defense-in-Depth

Six interconnected security and accessibility systems working in seamless synergy.

🎙️

Conversational Voice AI

Natural speech comprehension with clear spoken confirmations. Every action is announced audibly, so you always know recipient, amount, and status.

👤

ECAPA-TDNN Voice Biometrics

Client-side 192-dimensional acoustic embedding extraction. Validates speaker identity before accepting sensitive financial commands, with zero raw audio storage.

👁️

Privacy Vision Defense

On-device MobileNet neural observer detection. Computes spatial bounding box areas and warns you instantly if an eavesdropper is peeking.

⚡

Deterministic Smart QR

Rigorous NPCI UPI Intent validator. Prevents parameter pollution attacks, validates fixed vs open QR amounts, and immunizes against XSS redirects.

⏱️

17-State Safety Engine

A deterministic monotonic state machine enforcing complete validation. Includes a 3-second grace undo window and tamper-proof immutable balance ledger.

✍️

Draw-to-PIN Authentication

Neural stroke recognizer allowing blind users to draw digits or gestures anywhere on the touchscreen without locating a tiny visual keyboard.

Acoustic Front-End

16 kHz mono PCM → 512-point Radix-2 FFT → Pre-Emphasis → 80 Log-Mel triangular filterbanks spanning 20 Hz to 8000 Hz.

Attentive Pooling (ASP)

Computes channel-wise attention-weighted mean (μ) and standard deviation (σ) into a compact 192-dim unit vector.

Noise SNR Gate

Calculates true Signal-to-Noise Ratio (dB). Noisy inputs are classified as UNCERTAIN rather than false impostors.
